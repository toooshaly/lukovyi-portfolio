### Project Goals

The goal of the project is to make the app easy to use, so customers can quickly manage their accounts, loans and deposits. The focus is on keeping personal and financial data secure, adding useful features like mobile payments and integration with other services, and providing 24/7 support to help users anytime they need it.

![Nexus Bank — Project Goals](/media/nexus/s1-goals-vis.webp)

### Wireframes

Wireframes are low-fidelity layouts that define the structure, layout and functionality before any visual detail. They focus on where key elements sit — not colours or styles. Wireframing was essential here: to visualise the app's user flow, ensure clear navigation, and align the design with user needs and business goals before moving into high-fidelity.

![Nexus Bank — Wireframes](/media/nexus/s2-wireframes-vis.webp)

### Typography, Colors, Icons & Components

This section defines the visual identity — font styles, sizes and the colour palette used across the app. Typography keeps things readable and consistent; the palette supports the experience and the brand's tone. Together with the icon set and component library, they make a cohesive, clear and usable interface.
