### Project Goals

The goal is an efficient, modern and user-friendly platform that gives fast access to essential information and streamlines everyday work. Built to current standards, it's accessible to everyone regardless of age or skill level — a useful resource for both managers and newcomers. By consolidating everything in one place, it improves usability and convenience for every employee.

### User Research

User research aimed to understand the behaviour, requirements and motivations of the people using the intranet — through observation, task analysis and more.

<div class="cs-findings cs-duo">
  <div class="cs-find">
    <span class="t">Problems</span>
    <p>An outdated design, unclear colour scheme and overall visual inconsistency made the intranet hard to navigate and unpleasant to use. The structure and navigation were unintuitive — inconsistent sections, no clear organisation — so finding information was inefficient. Search was limited, outdated content lingered across pages, and essential tools like the phone directory and links were non-functional.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-find">
    <span class="t">Solutions</span>
    <p>Rebuild the intranet with a modern, clean design and a clear colour scheme to lift usability and satisfaction. Give it an intuitive navigation structure with consistent sections and a stronger search — interactive and keyword-based. Fix the specific broken tools, keep content updated, and streamline sections into one cohesive, accessible and efficient platform.</p>
  </div>
</div>
