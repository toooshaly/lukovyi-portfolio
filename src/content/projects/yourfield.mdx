### Project Goals

Develop an intuitive app to automate and optimize agricultural processes through AI, drone, and machine learning technologies. This app will support farmers in better field management, boosting crop yields, quickly detecting crop diseases, and enhancing decision-making through real-time data analysis.

![Project goals — field dashboard](/media/yourfield/goals.webp)

### Problem & Solution

Modern agriculture faces challenges in resource optimization and automation. Our solution integrates drones, GPS, and artificial intelligence to increase efficiency and reduce costs.

### App Architecture

Creating an app architecture was necessary to ensure an efficient and flexible application structure. It lets various functional modules — drone management, sensors, analytics — integrate into a single system. A well-designed architecture ensures scalability, stability and easy future updates, which keeps the application running efficiently as it grows.

![App Architecture — site map, drone and sensor modules](/media/yourfield/architecture-vis.webp)

### User Research

User research was conducted to better understand the needs and problems of the people who would use the app. It surfaced valuable insights that helped shape an intuitive interface and features that genuinely meet the target audience — making the product convenient, efficient and matched to the real needs of farmers and agricultural companies.
